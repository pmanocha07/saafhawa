Cheap wins that actually help

Skip agarbatti and mosquito coils. One coil can release as much PM2.5 as dozens of cigarettes, right inside your room.
Wet-mop, don't sweep. Dry sweeping lifts settled dust straight back into the air you breathe.
DIY purifier. A box fan with a MERV 13 filter taped to the back gives real CADR for a fraction of the price.
Seal the gaps. Weather strips and door snakes lower infiltration, so a purifier works far better.
Run it closed. A purifier in a room with an open door is cleaning the whole house, badly.
Masks outdoors. A well-fitted N95 cuts PM2.5 a lot; a loose cloth mask barely helps.

How it works

In Delhi the AQI is "Poor" or worse for weeks. Telling people that every day changes nothing. What does change is when: PM2.5 often swings two to three times within a single day. At night the ground cools, the mixing layer sinks to a few hundred metres and traps smoke. By afternoon the sun lifts it past a kilometre and dilutes it. SaafHawa forecasts that swing for your exact location and plans around it.

Method and honesty

Data. Hourly PM2.5 and PM10 forecasts from Open-Meteo, based on the Copernicus CAMS atmospheric model, plus mixing-layer height and wind. Model data covers every town in India, including the many with no CPCB station.

Index. The NAQI shown is an estimate: 24-hour average PM2.5 and PM10 converted with CPCB breakpoints. It will differ from your nearest station reading.

Planner. For each activity, every allowed start hour is scored by mean forecast PM2.5 over its duration. Inhaled dose = concentration × breathing rate × time.

Indoor. Steady-state mass balance: indoor = 0.8 × leak rate × outdoor ÷ (leak rate + 0.2 deposition + CADR ÷ room volume). Real rooms vary; treat it as a guide.

Limits. The model grid is about 40 km, so it can miss very local sources like a burning garbage dump next door. Forecast skill drops after about 48 hours.
